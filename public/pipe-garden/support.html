Pipe Garden Support

Something not working, or an idea for the game? Email atdhe@oxadevelopments.com. Please say which phone you use and which level you're on.

Will I lose my progress if I get a new phone?

Progress is saved on your device, not on our servers. Your phone's own backup usually brings it across when you restore to a new phone. Deleting the game without a backup deletes your progress.

I'm stuck on a level

Every level can be solved. Hold a pipe to turn it the other way, and use the wrench power-up to fix a pipe for you. Out of moves, or stuck for a long time? The game offers more moves and a way to skip ahead.

How do I change my ad choices?

Where the law requires asking, you can change your choice under Settings, Ad privacy choices. On iPhone, tracking is under Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. On Android, your advertising ID is under Settings, Privacy, Ads.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Everything works without a connection, except ads you choose to watch for a reward.

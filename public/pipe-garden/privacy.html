Pipe Garden Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Pipe Garden is a puzzle game made by OXA Developments. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

What the game keeps on your device

Your progress, stars, purchases made with stars and settings are saved on your device. We don't run servers for the game, you don't need an account, and we never receive this information. Your device's own backup may include it. Deleting the game deletes it from your device.

Ads

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier, and other device and app identifiers
  • your IP address and approximate location based on it
  • information about your device and how you interact with ads
  • diagnostic information, such as crashes in the ad software

Google uses this as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland, and other places that require it: the game asks for your choice before personalized ads are shown. You can change it any time under Settings, Ad privacy choices.
  • iPhone: the game asks permission before your device's advertising identifier can be used for tracking. You can change this in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings, under Privacy, Ads.

If you decline, you'll still see ads, but they'll be less personalized. The game plays the same either way.

Children

Pipe Garden is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. The game itself collects no personal information; the ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
